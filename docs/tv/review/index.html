Workout TV

Review the demo videos

  1. Tap a video to load it, and watch it with the sound on.
  2. Trim it: tap Start here where the demo starts and End here where it ends. Keep it short; the TV stops at anyway.
  3. Mark it Good or Bad. Check the backup too if the first one is bad. Paste a link if you find a better video.
  4. Tap Send results at the bottom and paste them to Claude in the Health project.

Your marks stay on this device until you send them.
